## Problem

A plating factory with **20** employees ran on verbal handoffs, paper logs, and no record of what happened to each order. That led to repeated quality disputes with its **40+** business customers.

## What I built

I went on-site, interviewed workers at every level, and found that the missing paper trail was the root cause. Then I built the factory's system on React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. Every order is tracked by LOT through each plating step. A customer portal shows the **40+** customers live order status. A built-in chatbot lets workers ask in their own language and lets managers ask for data in plain words. Workers now photograph the handwritten status boards at **10** stations and AI reads them. Slack agents handle sales reports and month-end emails, and I trained **3** key staff to run it all without me.

## Architecture

The app runs on AWS. Photos go to S3, an OCR step reads the numbers into the production log, and the chatbot answers from the same database. The customer portal reads from the same data, so there is one source of truth. Errors are watched with Sentry.

```
React app → Node.js API → PostgreSQL → S3 photos → AI OCR → production log → chatbot and customer portal
```

## Result

- Office team: **3 people to 1 manager**
- **1+ hour** of messenger replies saved daily, plus **1 hour** of floor walking
- Cloud cost: **-90%** after moving from Lambda to EC2
- A second factory with **50** employees signed through my own outreach
