## Problem

Professional photo shoots cost too much and book out for weeks, which is overkill for people who just want good portraits. Rental photo booths were mostly plastic, clashed with wood-accented Canadian venues, and still cost a premium.

## What I built

I launched Western Canada's first self-portrait studio in **2022**: a walk-in studio with no photographer, with sessions starting at CA$75. I built the whole booking site alone on React and Square checkout, with online booking, payment, and automated session handling. For events I designed and built wooden photo booths myself, which let us price about **30-40%** below typical rentals. An AI support chatbot, built on LangChain and a local model with RAG, cut the time spent answering customers. A team of **10** part-time staff ran sessions and events.

## Architecture

One website handles booking, payment, and session setup, so a customer can walk in and shoot without help from staff. The booths are separate hardware that I built and set up on-site, with the same software running them.

```
React booking site → Square checkout → automated session flow → studio and wooden photo booths
```

## Result

- **3,000+** self-portrait sessions
- **300+** photo booth events
- Monthly revenue of **$8-10K**, with **10** part-time staff and no full-time photographers
- Business sold to another operator

## Next

The business now runs under its new owner. I would apply the same self-serve model to other service businesses.
